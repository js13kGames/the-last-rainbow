# THE LAST RAINBOW

**The world has lost its colors. You are the last one who can bring them back.**

THE LAST RAINBOW is a fast-paced browser adventure where you journey through a fading world to restore the colors that have disappeared.

Explore a mysterious landscape, overcome obstacles, collect lost colors, and push forward through a world that slowly comes alive as you progress. Every color you recover changes the atmosphere and brings hope back to the world.

But you are running out of time.

🌈 Features

* **Color-based adventure** in a mysterious fading world
* **Fast and simple controls** designed for instant play
* **Exploration and obstacle-based gameplay**
* **Progressive visual transformation** as colors return
* **Atmospheric music and effects**
* **Lightweight browser gameplay** built for the js13kGames challenge
* **No installation required**

### Your Mission

Find the lost colors.

Restore the world.

Reach the end.

**You are the last hope.**

**Can you bring back the rainbow?**
